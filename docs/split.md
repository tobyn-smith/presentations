# Who speaks and when

The discussion is designed to run for 15 minutes. Keep to the handovers; there are three short live activities.

| Time | Speaker | Boards | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-6:00 | Tobyn | Opening through the sample-selection check | Set up the problem, run three short activities, explain the growth comparison and hand over |
| 6:00-9:00 | Raquel | Skocpol, Figure 4 and Figure 5 | Explain the causal sequence, show the contrast cases, then qualify the wider test |
| 9:00-15:00 | Tobyn | Chile, final interaction and close | Show how the endpoint changes the story, take the third vote with reasons, and connect the paper to our own research designs |

## Three short interactions

1. **Five growers (1:30-3:30):** sort what the selected cases show from what they do not establish.
2. **Choosing cases (5:10-6:00):** choose what should be added to make the growth claim a fairer test. The strongest answer is slower-growing countries.
3. **Which choice matters most? (11:00-13:15):** choose the case-selection or measurement decision that most changes the inference, then give a reason.

Do not run the old five-minute pairs activity. It would push the discussion beyond the 15-minute limit.

## Handover

Tobyn hands to Raquel at 6:00 after the case-selection vote. Raquel hands back at 9:00 after the Figure 5 qualification. Keep both handovers to one sentence.
